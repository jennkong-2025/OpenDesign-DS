Foundations

Our experiences are grounded in human needs, not system constraints. We design for emotional safety, comprehension, and trust across every ability, context, and state of mind.

01

Conversational

Write like a real person talking about healthcare, not a healthcare system talking at people.

02

Easy

Organize information so members can understand it without working through unnecessary complexity.

03

Practical

Guide members toward what they need and help them make informed decisions about their care.

04

Inclusive

Use respectful, accurate language that welcomes people across abilities, identities, and contexts.

Writing

Content helps members make informed decisions and find the care they need in the easiest way possible. Voice stays consistent; tone adapts to the person and moment.

Strategy statement

Empower people to self-serve, then guide them to care when they need support.
01

Be clear

Use concise, everyday language that a wide audience can understand.

02

Be concise

Keep the essential message visible and use white space to make it scannable.

03

Be consistent

Reuse established terms and patterns so the experience feels reliable.

Accessible writing

Accessibility and inclusivity go hand in hand. Familiar interactions and perceivable content make care information more usable for everyone.

Write for people across abilities and contexts

  • Prioritize simplicity and familiar, consistent interactions.
  • Make content perceivable through sight, hearing, or touch.
  • Use the language people and communities use for themselves.
  • Avoid language that frames disability as a negative quality.
Writing tip

Lead with the person’s goals and accomplishments before mentioning a disability when that detail is relevant.
